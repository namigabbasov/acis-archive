About the Archive

A record of ACIS's history and advocacy

This digital archive brings together historical materials associated with the American Committee for Interoperable Systems, including meeting notes, correspondence, amicus briefs, and formal comments. Materials are organized to support research into the organization's activities and its role in debates over software interoperability.
