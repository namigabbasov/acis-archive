About the American Committee for Interoperable Systems

The American Committee for Interoperable Systems (ACIS) was an organization involved in advocacy related to the interoperability of computer systems and software. Detailed historical background on the organization's founding, membership, and activities has not yet been finalized for this prototype and will be supplied ahead of the production launch.

02

About the Digital Archive

The ACIS Digital Archive is being developed by the Robert Crown Law Library at Stanford Law School to make historical materials associated with ACIS available for research. In production, the archive will be built on Omeka Classic, with digitized materials hosted in the Stanford Digital Repository (SDR).

03

About the Collection

The collection is organized into five material types: amicus briefs, meeting notes, letters, comments, and other organizational records. Together these materials document the committee's advocacy work and internal deliberations. Collection-level descriptions shown throughout this prototype are illustrative and will be refined by library staff prior to launch.

04

Access and Use

Access and use terms for the ACIS collection — including any restrictions on reproduction or citation requirements — will be published here once finalized by the Robert Crown Law Library. Researchers with questions in the meantime should use the Contact page.
